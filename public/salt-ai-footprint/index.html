Ways to offset it

Solar on a SALT project or the studio

Cuts emissions at the source

Our energy use is small enough that the yearly total is about the output of one panel. It is the easiest option to measure, because the meter shows what was produced. The counts above show the panel equivalent.

Colorado Tree Coalition, ReForest Colorado

Local, direct donation

The grant program replants communities after wildfire and other disasters. It runs on donations at coloradotrees.org. It gives local and community benefit, but it reports trees, not tonnes of carbon, so it works as a co-benefit gift and not a carbon credit.

Tree-Nation

Priced per tonne, certified projects

Offers projects under the Verified Carbon Standard and Plan Vivo, from about $7.50 to $50 a tonne (€6.67 to €45) and from 2 to 33 trees per tonne depending on the project. Its page does not describe how long the trees are protected, so ask before buying.

Engineered removal

Permanent, much higher price

Direct air capture and similar methods cost far more per tonne than tree credits, according to Evertreen. They lock carbon away for a long time. Worth pricing when permanence matters more than cost.

What to keep in mind

Read this as a first pass.
  • Anthropic has not published per-session energy, so the kWh range is the widest part of this estimate. Published analyses put their own uncertainty at about a factor of two each way.
  • Only data-center electricity is counted. Training the model, building chips and data centers, and our own laptops and networks are left out.
  • Trees take up carbon slowly at first, so planting now does not cancel this year's use this year. A tree also has to survive and stay standing for the full span.
  • Forest credits have a mixed record. A study of California's forest offsets found systematic over-crediting in one project type, so the credit type and verifier matter.
  • Solar avoids emissions that would otherwise happen. A tree removes carbon already emitted. They are different things and shouldn't be added together.
